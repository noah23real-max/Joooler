Performance OS

Privacy Policy

Performance OS is a local-first performance application designed to help strength athletes organize training, recovery, bodyweight, imported fitness data, and AI-assisted coaching.

Effective: September 5, 2026 Local-first architecture No advertising No sale of personal data

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Performance OS handles information when you use the application and when you connect third-party services such as WHOOP.

Performance OS is currently designed as a desktop application with local canonical storage. Most application data is stored on the user's own device rather than in a Performance OS cloud account.

2. Information Performance OS may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, Performance OS may process:

  • training history, exercises, sets, repetitions, load, RPE/RIR, and training plans;
  • bodyweight observations and related image metadata when you explicitly import them;
  • recovery information such as heart rate variability, resting heart rate, recovery score, sleep, strain, respiratory rate, SpO₂, or skin temperature when available from connected sources;
  • profile information you explicitly provide to the application;
  • conversation content and coaching requests entered into Performance OS;
  • technical provenance, revision history, sync status, and error information needed to keep imported data auditable and consistent.

3. WHOOP data

If you connect a WHOOP account, Performance OS requests only the permissions needed for the current recovery integration:

  • read:profile — basic profile identification required to verify the connection;
  • read:cycles — cycle and strain information;
  • read:recovery — recovery information including HRV and resting heart rate;
  • read:sleep — sleep sessions and sleep-related metrics.

Performance OS may also request the OAuth offline scope so that an authorized connection can be refreshed without asking you to sign in every time.

The current WHOOP recovery integration does not request WHOOP workout or body-measurement permissions unless a future version explicitly adds those features and asks for additional consent.

4. How WHOOP data is used

WHOOP data is used to:

  • display and analyze recovery-related information inside Performance OS;
  • create local recovery trends and compact recovery summaries;
  • reconcile updated or rescored WHOOP records without silently overwriting historical evidence;
  • support training and recovery decisions requested by the user.
WHOOP-imported measurements are treated as imported evidence. Performance OS keeps imported provider data distinct from values calculated by the application and from information inferred by AI features.

5. Local storage and credentials

Canonical Performance OS data is stored locally in the application's SQLite database on the user's device.

Sensitive credentials such as a WHOOP OAuth access token, refresh token, or provider client secret are intended to be stored in the operating system's secure credential storage/keyring, not in the canonical athlete database or browser localStorage.

6. AI features and third-party providers

Performance OS may include optional AI-assisted features. When you explicitly use such a feature, information relevant to your request may be sent to the AI provider configured in Performance OS in order to produce the requested response.

Performance OS is designed to send compact, relevant context rather than indiscriminately forwarding all locally stored data. The privacy practices of an external AI provider are also governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

7. Other connected services

If you connect another service, such as a training-history provider, Performance OS may retrieve data that you have authorized that service to provide. Imported records remain associated with their source so the application can distinguish imported data from user-entered observations and calculated values.

8. Sharing and sale of personal information

Performance OS does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

Data is transmitted to external services only when required for a feature you choose to use, such as connecting WHOOP or invoking a configured AI provider.

9. Retention and deletion

Local application data remains on your device until it is deleted through Performance OS, removed with the application data, or otherwise deleted by you.

Disconnecting WHOOP removes the local authorization token and Performance OS will attempt to revoke provider access when supported. Previously imported records may remain locally so that historical training and recovery data is not silently destroyed. You may remove local Performance OS data separately.

10. Security

Performance OS uses a local-first architecture, operating-system credential storage for sensitive provider credentials, provenance tracking, and versioned records to reduce accidental disclosure, silent overwrite, and data-integrity problems.

No software or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect access to their operating-system account and device.

11. Your control

You control whether third-party services are connected to Performance OS.

  • You may choose not to connect WHOOP.
  • You may disconnect WHOOP from Performance OS.
  • You may revoke Performance OS access from your WHOOP account/provider controls.
  • You may stop using optional AI features without disabling the local application.

12. Children's privacy

Performance OS is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Performance OS adds new integrations, changes how data is processed, or needs to clarify its privacy practices. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy materially changes.

14. Contact

Privacy questions about Performance OS should be sent to the contact email listed for the Performance OS application in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard.

15. WHOOP relationship

Performance OS is an independent application and is not endorsed by or affiliated with WHOOP. WHOOP data access is subject to the user's authorization and WHOOP's applicable platform terms and privacy practices.
